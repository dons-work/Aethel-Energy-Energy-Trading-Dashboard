Dashboard Overview
The European Energy Trading Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of key macroeconomic indicators, energy market fundamentals, and simulated real-time price movements across Europe's principal trading hubs. Designed for rapid insight and executive-level clarity across gas, power, carbon, and storage markets. This dashboard demonstrates core analytical methods, visual design principles, and the ability to translate complex market data into actionable strategic intelligence.
